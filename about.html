Data Deletion Request

At Dual N-Back, we value your privacy. Our app requires user accounts tied with Google sign in or local storage. We store all game related data to store your progress and show you statistics. We may collect minimal technical data (such as device identifiers or analytics) to improve your experience.

How to request data deletion

If you would like to request the deletion of any data associated with your use of the app, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to: chaos1618.dev@gmail.com
  2. Subject line: Data Deletion Request - Dual N-Back
  3. In the body of the email, please include your Device ID (if known) or any specific information that helps us identify your data.

What data is deleted?

Upon receiving a valid request, we will delete all identifiable technical logs, analytics data, and support history associated with your device within 30 days. We do not retain any personal data beyond what is necessary for legal or security purposes.
